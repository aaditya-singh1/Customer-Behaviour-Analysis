## 📌 Executive Summary & Key KPIs

* **Total Revenue:** ₹18.6M Net Sales[cite: 1]

* **Unique Customers:** 2,500[cite: 1]
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹2,210[cite: 1]

* **Purchase Frequency:** 3.37 orders / customer[cite: 1]
* **90-Day Retention Rate:** 68.4%[cite: 1]
* **Revenue Trend:** +52.6% growth from January through August[cite: 1]

## 🎯 RFM Customer Segmentation & Business Strategy

Using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) modeling, customers were categorized into 5 actionable segments[cite: 1]:

| Segment | Share | Revenue | Key Strategy / Action |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Champions** | 18% | ₹5.8M | Exclusive offers, early access, and premium VIP support[cite: 1]. |
| **Loyal Customers** | 24% | ₹4.9M | Loyalty programs and upselling strategies[cite: 1]. |
| **Potential Loyalists** | 27% | ₹4.1M | Personalized bundles and next-purchase incentives[cite: 1]. |
| **At Risk** | 17% | ₹2.3M | Win-back campaigns triggered by recency thresholds[cite: 1]. |
| **Hibernating** | 14% | ₹1.5M | Low-cost re-engagement discount offers[cite: 1]. |

## 🛠 Data Pipeline & Methodology

1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Python):**
   * Removed 142 duplicate order records[cite: 1].
   * Addressed 37 missing customer IDs[cite: 1].
   * Corrected 19 invalid quantities and standardized 100% of dates to ISO format[cite: 1].
   * Validated 64 outlier order values[cite: 1].

2. **Data Extraction & Analytics (SQL):**
   * Built analytical queries using Joins, CTEs, and Aggregations to compute KPIs and monthly performance trends[cite: 1].

3. **Dashboard & Visualization (Power BI):**
   * Built an interactive Customer Intelligence Dashboard with DAX metrics and dynamic filters (Date, Category, City, Segment)[cite: 1].
